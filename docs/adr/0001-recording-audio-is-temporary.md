# Recording audio is temporary; the clean Transcript is the record

A lecture Recording's audio is deleted automatically as soon as the final pass has produced its Transcript, and no raw transcript is kept: the cleaned Transcript (fillers out, sentences completed, terms corrected) is the lasting record. The student doesn't want audio kept anywhere, and the feature is for learning, not archiving. This departs from Almanac's rule that everything traces to its source: an extracted date or deadline can no longer be replayed, so anything plan-relevant that the transcription leaves unclear is asked about in chat instead of trusted, and only what's left uncertain is marked in the text.

## Considered options

- Keep audio until the quarter ends, so a quote could be replayed: rejected by the student (storage, privacy).
- Keep the raw transcript beside the clean one as the evidence: rejected; the clean text plus chat questions is the record they want.
- Delete each live chunk as soon as it's captioned (nothing on disk at all): rejected, since the final pass over the whole audio is the main accuracy step.
