# 04: Inbox: Proposals and Questions

**What to build:** Every assistant-originated change is a Proposal with its Source quote. The Inbox lists Proposals and Questions; the user accepts, edits then accepts, rejects, or accepts all from one Source. Answering a Question records the answer. Rejected Proposals are fingerprinted and not re-proposed. An Inbox count shows on every screen.
